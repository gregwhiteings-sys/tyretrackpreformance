Repairs
1. Best-day history is aggregated inside SQLite. Modern history rows are no
   longer copied into Python and converted one by one at every refresh.
2. Live totals use grouped database queries rather than reading each tyre
   from today's records into Python. Legacy timestamp compatibility retained.
3. Full historical statistics refresh at most once per five seconds; requests
   are not queued while a collection is already running. Live counters retain
   their independent event-triggered refresh and one-second periodic fallback.
4. Scrolling text uses fractional positioning and a precise 33 ms timer.
5. Logs include live-read time, queued delivery time and maximum marquee
   callback gap to help separate database work from GUI/remote delays.
6. Window title identifies Performance update 2026-10-29.

Kept from previous update
Weight pie charts in management email/PDF, count/weight/EPU totals, periodic
CSV exports and final pending CSV request at close. Camera quality, AI models,
recording, crossing rules, schedules and report calculations are unchanged.
Statistics such as historical best-day and weekday blocks may lag up to five
seconds; the headline counters use the independent lightweight read.
