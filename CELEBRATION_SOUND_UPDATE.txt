RANDOM CELEBRATIONS — UPDATE

Admin Settings > Performance:
Select Random celebration (shuffle all sounds), uncheck Mute celebrations,
and press Test selected celebration sound several times.
Each available sound plays once per shuffled cycle. The next choice and last
successful sound are saved across restarts. Failed playback does not consume
a choice. Missing files are excluded. The dashboard shows the actual selection.

ADD SOUNDS
Press Add celebration sound (WAV). Choose uncompressed 16-bit PCM WAV, mono
or stereo, up to 60 seconds and 12 MB. Short 3–10 second clips work well.
Files are copied to data/celebration_sounds_v2 beside the app and join Random.
No GitHub rebuild is needed for later WAV additions. Convert MP3 to PCM WAV;
renaming its extension does not convert it.
Preserve your existing data folder when upgrading. Do not replace site data
with test data. Imported files and existing history stay in your data folder.
