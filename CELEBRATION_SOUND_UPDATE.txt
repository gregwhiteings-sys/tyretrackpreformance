BEST-HOUR ALERT

This build contains one celebration alert: the audio supplied for this update. It plays only when a new best hour is reached. Daily-total records remain visible on the dashboard but do not play audio.

Open Admin Settings > Performance to mute/unmute the alert or press Test best-hour alert. Operational foreign-object alarms keep priority and block the celebration alert while active.

The WAV is bundled in the Windows build, so it does not depend on a separate audio file in the data folder.
