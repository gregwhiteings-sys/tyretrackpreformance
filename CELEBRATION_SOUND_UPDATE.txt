BEST-HOUR CELEBRATION SOUNDS

The previous Like Wooh Wooh recording has been removed. This build bundles all 21 clips from Voicy_R4V3 B0Y .zip, converted to compact 22.05 kHz mono, 16-bit PCM WAV files.

Random shuffle is selected by default. Every supplied clip plays once per shuffle cycle, with no back-to-back repeat. Automatic playback is still limited to new best-hour records; daily-total records do not play audio.

Open Admin Settings > Performance to mute/unmute celebrations, choose a clip, or press Test celebration sound. Operational foreign-object alarms keep priority and block celebration playback while active.

All WAV files are bundled with the Windows build. The recordings use approximately 8 MB in the source tree and do not add camera, inference, database, or network work.
