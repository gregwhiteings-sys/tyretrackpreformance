TYRETRACK - WEIGHT REPORT CHART AND LIVE COUNTER UPDATE
28 October 2026

Weight reporting
- Daily and weekly management emails include an inline estimated-weight pie chart.
- Their attached PDFs retain the tyre-count mix chart and add a separate weight mix chart.
- Passenger = 8 kg, SUV / 4x4 = 16 kg, truck = 45 kg per counted tyre.
- Pie percentages use kilograms. Unclassified tyres have no assigned weight.
- CSV totals, EPU factors and reporting schedules are unchanged.
- Included report preview uses demonstration data.

Counter responsiveness
- Committed counts trigger a separate background read for Today, This Hour,
  all-time counts, class totals and estimated kg/EPU.
- Heavy historical dashboard queries no longer determine when those labels update.
- Existing slow statistics refreshes cannot overwrite the fast headline labels.
- CSV exports run at most roughly every ten seconds during busy counting, with
  a pending final export requested on app close. Counts remain saved in SQLite.
- Camera pipeline, models, count crossing rules and production schedule unchanged.
