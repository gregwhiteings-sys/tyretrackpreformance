TYRETRACK SECOND CAMERA SETUP

Dashboard
- The dashboard displays Camera 1 and Camera 2 side by side.
- Camera 1 is the designated counting camera.
- Camera 2 is a verification view at 480x270 and 5 fps. It runs no tyre detector, tracker, recording pipeline, or database writer.

Connect Camera 2
1. Connect the second OAK-D to the PC and power/network.
2. Open Admin Settings > Camera.
3. Under SECOND OAK-D, click Find OAK cameras.
4. Select the serial number belonging to the second physical camera, then click Connect Camera 2.
5. The selected serial is saved and reconnects on the next app launch. Use Disconnect to stop its preview.

Counting and duplicate protection
Production totals are generated only by Camera 1's existing unique crossing logic. Camera 2 does not add counts. This avoids double counting when the same tyre appears in both views. A true cross-camera merged counter requires the installed camera positions, overlapping field of view, and measured transit delay to be calibrated first; this clip shows only the existing single-camera screen, so those values are not known yet.

Performance
The secondary pipeline streams a single 480x270 preview at 5 fps. It has no neural inference or recording workload. Camera 1's detection, tracking, recording, and count logic are unchanged.

Best-hour sound
The supplied Like Wooh Wooh recording is the only celebration alert. It plays for a new best hour; a daily total record alone remains visual and does not play it. Admin Settings > Performance has a test button and mute control. Operational alarms retain priority.
